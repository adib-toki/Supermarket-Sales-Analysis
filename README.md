### 🛍️ Product Line Performance

**Lowest Performing Product Line:** Food and Beverages

### 💳 Payment Method

**Most Used Payment Method:** Cash

## 🔍 Main Observations

1. **Branch B generated the highest revenue** among the three branches, making it the strongest revenue contributor in this dataset.

2. **Sports and Travel generated the highest revenue** among all product lines, indicating strong revenue contribution from this category.

3. **Food and Beverages had the highest quantity sold**, showing that it was the most frequently purchased product line by sales volume, despite generating the lowest total revenue.

4. **Cash was the most-used payment method**, with more orders recorded through Cash than Credit Card or Ewallet.

## 📈 Key Business Takeaways

* Branch performance varies significantly, with Branch B contributing the highest revenue.
* Revenue performance and sales volume do not always follow the same pattern. Food and Beverages had the highest quantity sold but the lowest revenue among product lines.
* Sports and Travel generated the highest product-line revenue.
* Cash remains the most frequently used payment method in the analyzed dataset.

## 🎯 Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of sales performance across branches, product lines, and payment methods. The findings can help the business identify high-revenue areas, understand customer purchasing behavior, and monitor differences between sales volume and revenue contribution.
